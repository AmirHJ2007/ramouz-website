About us

The story behind Ramouz.

01 The story

Two brothers, one idea

Ten years ago, two brothers opened a small café in Mashhad. Nine more followed. The tenth is here, on the Muscat waterfront.

02 The craft

Roasted by us, baked with love

We started out pouring Lavazza. Now we import the beans, roast them under our own name, and bake everything in the case ourselves.

03 The company

More than a café

We supply Italian espresso machines and barista gear to cafés across Iran. We just kept the best of it for ourselves.

04 The school

Ramouz School

Coffee is a craft, and we teach it. From your first pour to your first shift behind a real bar — hands-on classes, real machines, and a certificate signed by the people who trained you.
