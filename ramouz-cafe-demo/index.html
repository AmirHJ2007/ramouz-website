About us

The story behind Ramouz.

A café, bakery, and roastery on the Muscat waterfront — specialty coffee, fresh bakes, and a playful cat that signs it all.

01 The story

Two brothers, one idea

Ten years ago in Iran, Ramouz was just an idea shared by two brothers — and one small café in Mashhad. Today that idea has grown into nine branches across Mashhad, and the newest chapter is here, on the Muscat waterfront.

02 The craft

Roasted by us, baked with love

We began by pouring Lavazza. Today we import our own beans and roast them under the Ramouz name — and every bake in our pastry case is made in-house, by our own hands, with love.

03 The company

More than a café

Ramouz is a company as much as a coffee shop. We import Italy's finest espresso machines and professional barista gear — and we brew on the very same equipment we supply, so every cup we hand you is the best we know how to make.

04 The school

Ramouz School

Coffee is a craft, and we teach it. Ramouz School takes you from your first pour to professional barista — hands-on classes, real bar time, and a valid certificate to carry your name forward.
